REFERENCE LAYOUT
LIBRARY
CHAPTER · 04

On the discipline of restraint

☆ Bookmark Annotate

A panel that holds its image without power invites a different kind of design. The interface is no longer a stream of frames; it is a page, set once and read calmly. Every state change is a small act of typesetting. Restraint is therefore not a stylistic preference but a hardware obligation: each transition consumes a full refresh, each gradient becomes a stipple, each shadow becomes a smear. The discipline of e-paper is the discipline of the press.
